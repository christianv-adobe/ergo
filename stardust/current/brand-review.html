Tensions

brand-review-template.md § Detector rules

T-scale

Type scale is ad-hoc (12 → 14 → 16 → 18 → 20 → 24 → 28 → 40px, no consistent ratio). Direct will need to decide whether the target adopts a modular scale — for a replica, keep it verbatim.

T-cta-vocab

CTA voice is fragmented: mehr lesen (6), Mehr lesen (3), Mehr erfahren (3), Jetzt informieren (5), Mehr Informationen (1). Direct will need to pick a canonical voice for see-more / read-more / learn-more affordances (replica: record in the inconsistency register only).

T-logo-variants

Logo variants captured: ERGO without claim (header mobile), ERGO with claim (header desktop), DKV co-brand. No monochrome / inverted variant exists on the site.

T-img-alt-empty

64% of images (307/483) carry empty alt text — mostly decorative icons in tiles. Accessibility and content-sourcing decision.

T-color-imbalance

Pastel surfaces #ffe0cb, #fef6d2, #f5e1eb, #ccecef, #d3ebe5, #ebe6d8 appear as background only — by design (style-system band classes).

agent: eyebrow-h1

The semantic h1 on every page is the small FS Me eyebrow; the large Fedra Serif claim is a <p>. Heading outline and visual hierarchy disagree — keep both roles in the replica.

agent: de-redirect

/de 301-redirects to /; index and de are the same page in state.json (de lacks the personalised "Ihre Versicherungen" block).
